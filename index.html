Everything descends from a single ancestor. Bodies carry genes (speed, size, sense, diet, colour) wiring a small neural net. Big organisms can eat smaller ones; mating only happens between similar colours, so lineages split into species; juveniles grow with age; brains can emit & smell pheromones. No behaviour is scripted.
